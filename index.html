UnivMyst_tiktok

Short animated videos about curiosities, science, mysteries and stories.

What it is. UnivMyst_tiktok is a desktop application used by the creator of the TikTok account @univmyst and the YouTube channel @UnivMyst to produce short animated videos and publish them to the creator's own accounts. On TikTok, before anything is posted the creator reviews the video, edits the caption, and chooses the privacy and interaction settings. On YouTube, only videos approved in the app are uploaded to the creator's own channel, and the app reads that channel's own statistics. Nothing is posted without the creator's approval. This app uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy).

Qué es. UnivMyst_tiktok es una aplicación de escritorio con la que el creador de la cuenta de TikTok @univmyst produce videos cortos animados y los publica en su propia cuenta. Antes de publicar, el creador revisa el video, edita el texto y elige la privacidad y los permisos. Nada se publica sin su confirmación expresa.
