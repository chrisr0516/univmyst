Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. What data the app uses

When you connect your TikTok account, the app receives, through TikTok's official APIs and only with your permission:

  • user.info.basic: your display name and avatar, to show which account will receive the post.
  • video.upload / video.publish: permission to send the videos you confirm to your account.
  • video.list: the public statistics of your own videos (views, likes, comments, shares), so you can see how they perform.

2. Where the data is stored

All data (access tokens, settings, video files and statistics) is stored only on the creator's own computer. The app has no server of its own and does not upload your data anywhere except to TikTok when you confirm a post.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is only exchanged with TikTok, as needed to post your confirmed videos and to read the statistics of your own videos.

4. Deleting your data

You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from the app ("Social networks → Remove") or from TikTok's settings ("Security and permissions → Apps"). Disconnecting deletes the stored access tokens from your computer.

5. Contact

Privacy questions: c2430100@gmail.com.
