Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

UnivMyst_tiktok ("the app") is a desktop application used by a single creator to publish short animated videos to the creator's own TikTok account and YouTube channel. The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is handled according to the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

1. What data the app uses

TikTok — when you connect your TikTok account, the app receives, through TikTok's official APIs and only with your permission:

  • user.info.basic: your display name and avatar, to show which account will receive the post.
  • video.upload / video.publish: permission to send the videos you confirm to your account.
  • video.list: the public statistics of your own videos (views, likes, comments, shares), so you can see how they perform.

YouTube (YouTube API Services) — when you connect your YouTube channel with Google's official sign-in, the app requests only:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the videos you approve to your own channel, with the title, description and tags you approve.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel's name and ID (to show which channel is connected) and the basic public data of the videos the app uploaded (title, privacy status, view/like/comment counts).
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read aggregated analytics of your own videos (views, watch time, audience retention) so the app can learn which topics and posting times work best for your channel.

The app does not access your email, contacts, subscriptions, comments of other users, or any channel you did not connect.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to (a) publish the videos you approve to your own accounts, (b) show you whether each post succeeded, and (c) show you statistics of your own videos and use them, on your computer, to suggest better topics and posting times. The data is never used for advertising, never sold and never combined with data from other users.

3. Where the data is stored

All data (access and refresh tokens, settings, video files and statistics) is stored only on the creator's own computer, in local files. The app has no server of its own and does not use cookies or tracking. Data only travels to TikTok, Google/YouTube, when you publish or when the app reads the statistics of your own videos.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is only exchanged with TikTok and Google/YouTube, as needed to post your approved videos and to read the statistics of your own videos.

5. Revoking access and deleting your data

  • In the app: "Social networks → Remove" disconnects an account and deletes its stored tokens from your computer immediately.
  • YouTube / Google: you can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • TikTok: "Settings → Security and permissions → Apps".

Statistics stored by the app are deleted or refreshed at least every 30 days, and all stored YouTube data is deleted when you disconnect the channel or revoke access. To request deletion by email, write to the contact below.

Política de privacidad (español)

UnivMyst_tiktok es una app de escritorio que usa un único creador para publicar sus videos en su propia cuenta de TikTok y en su propio canal de YouTube. La app usa los Servicios de API de YouTube: al conectar un canal aceptas los Términos de servicio de YouTube y se aplica la Política de privacidad de Google.

Con tu permiso y por las API oficiales, la app recibe: de TikTok, tu nombre y avatar, el permiso para enviar los videos que confirmes y las estadísticas públicas de tus propios videos; de YouTube, el permiso para subir los videos que apruebes (youtube.upload), el nombre e ID de tu canal y los datos básicos de los videos que subió (youtube.readonly) y las estadísticas de tus propios videos, como vistas y retención (yt-analytics.readonly). Solo se usa para publicar lo que apruebas y para mostrarte y aprender de los resultados de tus videos; nunca para publicidad. Todo se guarda solo en el ordenador del creador (sin servidor propio ni cookies). No vendemos ni compartimos tus datos.

Puedes desconectar tus cuentas desde la app (se borran los permisos guardados) o retirar el acceso en la página de seguridad de Google (security.google.com/settings/security/permissions) y en los ajustes de TikTok. Las estadísticas guardadas se borran o actualizan como mínimo cada 30 días. Contacto: c2430100@gmail.com.
